# The Garden Behind the School — visual continuity and scene specifications

## Story-wide continuity bible

- **Yael:** 15-year-old Israeli student; olive skin, dark-brown curly hair tied in a low ponytail, round green eyeglasses, long-sleeve sage-green shirt, ankle-length dark denim skirt, grey gardening gloves when working.
- **Maya:** 15-year-old Israeli student; light-brown skin, straight black hair in a long braid, mustard long-sleeve shirt, loose navy trousers, blue gardening gloves when working.
- **Daniel:** 15-year-old Israeli student; olive skin, short dark hair, dark-blue knitted kippah, pale-blue long-sleeve shirt, dark trousers, brown gardening gloves when working.
- The three students retain exactly the same faces, age, body shape, hair, clothes and identifying accessories in all scenes. Yael's green eyeglasses must remain on her face in every scene; they are never removed or duplicated.
- **Location:** one unused rectangular corner immediately behind the same pale-Jerusalem-stone school building. A blue-grey service door, two high windows, a low stone boundary wall and one mature olive tree beyond the wall establish the same location in every scene.
- **Garden plan:** drought-tolerant native Israeli plants only: red anemones, purple sage, yellow rockrose and small rosemary shrubs. Pale gravel paths, several larger existing stones reused as borders, a small wooden bench, mulch around plants and a thin black drip-irrigation line. No lawn, fountain or water-hungry tropical plants.
- **Style:** cinematic storybook realism for teenagers, portrait mobile composition, natural proportions, realistic school project rather than a fantasy transformation.
- **Time arc:** scene 1 is before work; scene 2 is planning at the same dry site; scene 3 is planting; scene 4 is several weeks later after modest, believable growth.
- **Never show:** unrelated furniture repair, workshop tools, indoor carpentry, changing school architecture, changing clothes or faces, text, labels, logos, water sprinklers, lush lawn, huge trees appearing instantly, childish cartoon styling or distorted hands.

## Scene 1 — The neglected area

**Sentence:** “An empty area behind the school was dry and full of stones.”

**Context:** Before the students begin, the corner has no garden and no furniture.

**Required details:** Show the pale-stone rear school wall, blue-grey service door and two high windows. The ground is compact dry tan soil with scattered stones of different sizes, sparse dead weeds and no healthy flowers. The low boundary wall and mature olive tree beyond it are visible. Yael, Maya and Daniel stand at the edge examining the space, still in their fixed clothing and without gardening gloves. Yael wears her green eyeglasses. They do not yet hold plans, plants or tools.

**Action/expression:** They look thoughtfully across the empty ground. Their expressions show assessment, not sadness or celebration.

**Composition/light:** Medium-wide portrait view that clearly establishes the entire problem area. Neutral late-morning light, dry muted palette.

**Do not show:** garden beds, bench, birds, flowers, drip line, watering, carpentry, chairs or a completed plan.

## Scene 2 — Designing a low-water garden

**Sentence:** “Students designed a small garden, using very little water.”

**Context:** The same day, before digging. This is a planning scene at the actual site, not construction and not an indoor workshop.

**Required details:** The same three students gather around a simple portable drawing board placed on a stable folding stand. A hand-drawn plan is visible only as non-readable shapes: small planting clusters, curved pale-gravel path, bench position and thin drip lines. Yael, still wearing green eyeglasses, points from the plan to the dry ground. Maya holds two small sample pots—one rosemary and one purple sage. Daniel holds a short coil of thin black drip-irrigation tubing and one low-flow emitter. Existing stones remain scattered because planting has not begun. No gardening gloves yet.

**Action/expression:** All three focus on the plan and exchange practical ideas. The connection between the plan, drought-resistant plants and limited irrigation must be visually obvious.

**Composition/light:** Both the plan and unchanged dry area are readable in one portrait frame. Slightly warmer daylight.

**Do not show:** wooden chairs, saws, hammers, repaired furniture, a finished garden, mature flowers, active sprinklers or text on the plan.

## Scene 3 — Planting and protecting the soil

**Sentence:** “They planted local flowers, and protected the soil.”

**Context:** Work has begun according to scene 2's plan. The garden is new and incomplete.

**Required details:** The same three students now wear their assigned gardening gloves. Yael, with green eyeglasses still on her face, kneels and plants a small red anemone seedling. Maya presses natural wood-chip mulch around a young purple sage plant. Daniel carefully lays the same thin black drip line beside a small rosemary shrub. Several existing stones have been moved into a low border that prevents soil erosion; other stones remain naturally present. A hand trowel and one small watering can sit safely on the ground, but no hose or sprinkler runs.

**Action/expression:** Concentrated, cooperative work. Hands interact naturally with soil, mulch, plant and drip line.

**Composition/light:** Close enough to identify each protective method—native plants, mulch, stone border and drip line—while retaining the school wall and service door for continuity.

**Do not show:** fully mature garden, bench occupied by people, birds yet, flooding, lawn, tropical flowers or workshop furniture.

## Scene 4 — A quiet living garden

**Sentence:** “The garden attracted birds, and gave everyone a quiet place.”

**Context:** Several weeks later. Growth is modest but clearly successful; the same location has become calm and useful.

**Required details:** The native red anemones, purple sage, yellow rockrose and rosemary are established but not unrealistically huge. Pale gravel forms a short path. The reused-stone borders, wood-chip mulch and thin black drip line remain visible. A small simple wooden bench now stands in the position shown in the scene-2 plan. Yael, Maya and Daniel sit calmly on or beside the bench with relaxed posture; Yael still wears her green eyeglasses. Two small local songbirds are present: one perched on the low stone wall and one near the flowering sage. They must be naturally scaled and not handled by students.

**Action/expression:** The students quietly observe the birds and garden. No one poses for the camera or celebrates dramatically.

**Composition/light:** Warm afternoon portrait scene, garden dominant, school wall/door and olive tree still confirming the same site.

**Do not show:** dense jungle, lawn, fountain, many exotic birds, bird cages, feeding birds by hand, changed clothing, an indoor scene or a formal public park.
