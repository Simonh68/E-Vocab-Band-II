# The Anonymous Account — continuity and eight-scene specification

## Fixed continuity

- **Amit:** 16, olive skin, short dark hair, dark knitted kippah, navy hoodie, beige trousers; one of the account creators.
- **Shira:** 16, light olive skin, long dark hair tied back, rust-coloured long-sleeve blouse, ankle-length navy skirt; second creator.
- **Lior:** 16, fair olive skin, curly brown hair, round black eyeglasses, pale-grey long-sleeve shirt, dark trousers; student harmed by the posts.
- **Counsellor:** woman about 38, muted-grey headscarf, light-grey cardigan, long charcoal skirt.
- Same faces, clothes, phones and school throughout. Phone screens use simple non-readable shapes/icons only—no generated text, usernames or legible posts.
- School: pale-stone Israeli religious high school, blue-framed classroom windows, same desks and counselling room.

## Episodes

1. **Anonymous account created:** Amit and Shira sit together after class, privately creating a humorous school account on one phone. Screen shows a generic masked profile silhouette and harmless joke-image shapes, no readable text. Lior is not present.
2. **Posts become personal; absence:** Amit and Shira look uneasy at a phone now showing a generic post layout with a cropped eyeglasses/curly-hair image resembling Lior. In the classroom, Lior’s usual seat is conspicuously empty with his closed notebook left beneath it; no duplicate Lior.
3. **Listening to opinions:** in counselling room, Amit, Shira, Lior and counsellor sit in a circle. Lior speaks with controlled hurt; the creators listen without phones in their hands; counsellor facilitates.
4. **Considering choices and results:** same room. A phone lies face-up at centre with four large non-readable option icons (delete, report, message, meeting). The group discusses consequences; counsellor gestures between options. No one has acted yet.
5. **Remove account and accept responsibility:** Amit presses a clear generic delete/trash icon on the phone while Shira openly acknowledges responsibility to Lior and counsellor. Their posture is accountable, not celebratory; Lior listens cautiously.
6. **First repair plan has problems:** Amit and Shira have sent a quick generic apology message, but Lior remains uncomfortable at his desk and classmates still glance at phones. The creators realize deletion and one message did not repair trust.
7. **Different method:** creators meet counsellor to plan direct restorative actions: face-to-face apology, speaking to classmates, reporting copied posts and checking on Lior. Show these as non-readable icon cards; all focus on a concrete plan.
8. **Repair requires more:** several days later, Amit and Shira help correct the situation in class and include Lior in a real group project. Phones are put away. Lior participates but expressions remain thoughtful rather than instantly joyful, showing trust rebuilding over time.

## Never show

Readable social-media text, brand logos, adult presented as Lior’s mother, changed glasses, changed clothes, a police scene, public humiliation, comedy celebration, physical violence, or a single phone-delete image reused for multiple episodes.
