# The Repair Café — continuity and eight-scene specification

## Fixed continuity

- Same community room in every indoor scene: pale stone walls, two large windows, long workbenches, pegboard of hand tools, labelled-looking but unreadable storage boxes; no written text or logos.
- **Tamar:** 16, olive skin, long dark braid, burgundy long-sleeve shirt, dark ankle-length skirt, clear safety glasses while repairing.
- **Eitan:** 16, olive skin, short dark hair, navy knitted kippah, beige long-sleeve shirt, dark trousers.
- **Mr Cohen:** 68, silver hair and short beard, blue knitted kippah, light-grey shirt, brown work apron.
- **Ruth:** 65, grey hair covered by a muted-blue headscarf, forest-green cardigan, long navy skirt, tan work apron.
- Same faces, clothes, room, green desk lamp with brass neck, and worn wooden chair throughout. Tools are used safely; teenagers and adults work side by side.

## Episodes

1. **Broken objects thrown away:** outside the community building, Tamar and Eitan look into a municipal collection area containing the same repairable green lamp and worn chair among a few discarded household objects. They recognize unnecessary waste; nothing is being repaired yet.
2. **Monthly workshop opens:** inside the fixed room, Tamar and Eitan welcome Mr Cohen and Ruth at prepared workbenches. The green lamp and chair are placed for inspection; pegboard tools are orderly. A calendar may be implied by one blank wall card but no legible writing.
3. **Listening to opinions:** all four stand around the lamp and chair. Mr Cohen points to the lamp’s cord; Ruth indicates a loose chair joint; teenagers listen and make eye contact, hands still, no repair started.
4. **Considering choices and results:** close planning moment: Eitan holds the unplugged lamp plug, Tamar compares two safe replacement components, while volunteers indicate the chair joint and a small parts tray. Their expressions show weighing options, not conflict.
5. **Repairing and sharing skills:** two coordinated work areas in one frame. Under Mr Cohen’s guidance, Eitan replaces the unplugged lamp cord; Ruth shows Tamar how to clamp and glue the chair joint. Safety glasses visible, no live electricity.
6. **First plan has problems:** the lamp still fails during a safe unplugged continuity test and the chair joint sits slightly misaligned in its clamp. The group notices calmly; no sparks, injury or dramatic failure. Previously used parts remain visible.
7. **Different method:** Mr Cohen shows Eitan a faulty internal switch and a multimeter with unreadable display; Ruth and Tamar reposition the chair clamp using a square guide. This is clearly a revised method based on scene 6.
8. **Reuse and relationships:** same repaired green lamp now glowing safely on a side table and same sturdy chair in use. Teenagers and volunteers share tea around the workbench, relaxed and connected; a small orderly group of other repaired objects suggests reduced waste.

## Never show

Playground cleanup, rubbish bags as the main activity after scene 1, changing characters or clothing, power tools used unsafely, sparks, injuries, readable writing, brand marks, unrelated school scenes, or the same composition repeated.
