Privacy

The project uses limited anonymous operational analytics to understand which vocabulary groups, stories and actions are useful and where learners stop.

Measured

The record may include the site and page, a short-lived visit identifier for one browsing session, broad actions such as opening a group, pressing a button, requesting audio, selecting an option, an anonymous correct or incorrect outcome, completion and approximate active time.

Not collected

The project does not collect names, email addresses, browser fingerprints, permanent personal identifiers, typed answers, voice recordings or student profiles. The project does not use first-party cookies for analytics.
