## Approved direction

Stage 6 integrates the approved adaptive Word Forge path into the existing full-screen Block Quest shell. It preserves the Stage 5 exponential ×1/×2/×4/×8 reward and the three pirate treasure chests while adding deeper vocabulary evidence.

## Learning path

- All 55 authentic Group 01 records form the source pool.
- Each mission contains 10 words; six consecutive missions rotate through the complete pool and then wrap.
- A word begins with meaning recognition and then moves to retrieval or context.
- Fast consecutive success opens retrieval; other successful recognition opens context.
- An error opens a non-punitive support bridge and returns the word after two intervening tasks.
- Two recent errors temporarily reduce the answer set from four choices to two.
- Mastery requires two correct signals from distinct learning depths.
- Progress stores only serial identifiers and signal names on the device; answers and learner identity are not stored or sent.
